# Original research

This collection is for original orbital engineering work. Use one folder per project for the manuscript or report, with the inputs, methods and replay instructions that support its findings.

## Studies

[Cooperative Spacecraft Docking with Time-Limited Readiness Guarantees](cooperative-docking/) — Adam Cohen. A contact guarantee for two spacecraft with independent clocks, with a worked counterexample and evidence from 57 simulated encounters comparing two timing policies.

The [reproduction index](../README.md#reproductions) lists studies that revisit results from published papers.
